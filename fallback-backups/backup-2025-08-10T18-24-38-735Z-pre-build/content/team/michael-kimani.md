Michael Kimani is our dedicated Safety Manager who ensures that all Monisa Construction projects maintain the highest safety standards. Michael's approach to safety management goes beyond compliance—he creates a culture where safety is everyone's responsibility. His proactive safety programs have earned recognition from industry organizations and have become a model for other construction companies in the region.

Michael believes that every worker has the right to return home safely each day. His comprehensive approach to safety includes regular training, hazard identification, and creating an environment where workers feel comfortable reporting safety concerns without fear of reprisal.

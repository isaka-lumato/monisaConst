# Residential Apartment Block Project

This modern apartment building provides quality housing solutions in the growing Kinondoni district. The 3-story building features 18 contemporary apartment units designed for modern urban living with community amenities and sustainable features.

## Project Overview

Completed in January 2024 after 14 months of construction, this residential apartment block addresses the growing demand for quality housing in Dar es Salaam. The building combines modern design with practical functionality to create an attractive living environment.

## Apartment Features

### Unit Configurations
- 12 two-bedroom, one-bathroom units (650 sq ft each)
- 6 three-bedroom, two-bathroom units (850 sq ft each)
- Open-plan living and dining areas
- Modern kitchens with built-in appliances
- Private balconies with city views

### Modern Amenities
- Central water supply with backup storage
- Reliable electrical systems with backup power
- High-speed internet infrastructure
- Cable TV preparation in all units
- Air conditioning preparation

## Building Design

### Architectural Features
- Contemporary facade with attractive brick and concrete design
- Large windows for natural lighting
- Covered walkways and common areas
- Landscaped entrance and courtyard
- Secure gated entry with intercom system

### Structural Excellence
- Reinforced concrete frame construction
- Earthquake-resistant design standards
- High-quality waterproofing systems
- Durable exterior finishes
- Sound insulation between units

## Community Amenities

### Shared Facilities
- Common recreation area with seating
- Children's play area in the courtyard
- Covered parking for residents
- Laundry facilities on each floor
- Storage areas for each unit

### Security and Safety
- 24-hour security system with cameras
- Controlled access entry points
- Emergency lighting and exit systems
- Fire safety equipment throughout
- Secure mailbox area

## Sustainable Features

### Environmental Considerations
- Rainwater collection for landscaping
- Energy-efficient lighting in common areas
- Natural ventilation design
- Waste separation and management system
- Native plant landscaping requiring minimal water

### Long-term Value
- Low-maintenance exterior materials
- Quality fixtures and fittings
- Efficient building systems
- Designed for easy maintenance and repairs

## Market Success

The apartment block achieved remarkable market success:
- All 18 units pre-sold during construction
- Premium pricing achieved due to quality and location
- Strong rental demand in the area
- Positive impact on neighborhood property values

### Project Management
